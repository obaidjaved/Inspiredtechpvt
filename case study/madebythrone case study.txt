Case Study: Custom Shopify Development for USA Beauty & Cosmetic Brand Made by Throne
Client Platform: madebythrone.com

Industry: Cosmetics, Beauty & Skincare

Core Technologies: Shopify 2.0, Custom Liquid, Tailwind CSS / JavaScript, Klaviyo, Meta / Google Tracking Infrastructure

Executive Summary
Made by Throne is a premium US-based beauty and cosmetic brand that required a custom, high-converting digital storefront built entirely from scratch. To capture the brand's aesthetic while driving conversion rates, a custom Shopify 2.0 architecture was engineered to highlight product textures, shade variations, and customer reviews. The outcome is a high-speed, mobile-optimized store designed for US beauty consumers.

1. The Challenge
High Aesthetic Standard: Cosmetic brands require photorealistic visual presentation, detailed ingredient callouts, and interactive shade selectors.

Mobile-First Shoppers: Over 80% of cosmetic traffic originates from mobile social channels (TikTok, Instagram), requiring instant page loads and frictionless single-swipe checkouts.

Custom Store Requirements: Default Shopify themes lacked the flexibility needed for custom bundle builders, dynamic cross-sells, and custom product page (PDP) storytelling sections.

2. Technical Architecture & Solutions
System Workflow:

Custom Front-End Storefront (Shopify 2.0 / Liquid)

Custom-coded Liquid sections built from scratch to allow full modular content control without code bloat or third-party theme limitations.

Interactive Product Discovery Engine

Built custom variant swatches, shade match visualizers, ingredient tabs, and rich before/after gallery sliders directly on the PDP.

Conversion & Retention Ecosystem

Integrated custom slide-out cart (Drawer Cart) featuring progress bars for free shipping, dynamic cross-sells, and single-click checkout routing.

3. Key Implementation Pillars
Bespoke Theme Development: Developed a clean, lightweight Shopify 2.0 theme from scratch, avoiding heavy third-party page builders to maximize site speed and SEO performance.

Custom PDP Architecture: Built tailored cosmetic sections including shade selectors, texture zoom features, customer reviews, and accordions for usage instructions.

Full E-Commerce Stack Setup: Configured payment gateways (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, credit cards), shipping logic for US domestic fulfillment, and tracking pixels for Meta and Google Ads.

4. Platform Metrics & System Impact
Lightning-Fast Mobile Performance: Custom Liquid architecture delivers rapid load speeds on mobile networks.

Increased Average Order Value (AOV): Dynamic slide-out cart with free shipping thresholds and cross-sell prompts increases cart size before checkout.

Zero Theme Bloat: Scratch-built code ensures total design freedom and effortless future feature updates.

5. Summary
By building Made by Throne’s storefront entirely from scratch on Shopify 2.0, the platform provides a high-end beauty experience tailored to the modern US cosmetic market—combining visual presentation with a conversion-focused checkout pipeline.
